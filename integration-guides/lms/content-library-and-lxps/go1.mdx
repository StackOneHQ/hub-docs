## **Summary**

The GO1 integration allows you to pull curated content from your customer's GO1 platform and display it in your LMS/LXP.

It integrates content, users, user progress and pulls in extensive metadata.

Customers can curate their own content library via (GO1 Admin)[https://developers.go1.com/products/go1-admin/]

### **Limitations**

* **Rate Limits:** No rate limits currently.
